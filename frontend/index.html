🚀 InspectAI Demonstration & Inspection Guide
🛡️
DECISION-SUPPORT NOTICE: InspectAI is an assistive decision-support platform designed to highlight spatial anomalies and retrieve technical guidance. Mahalanobis anomaly scores represent spatial distance from learned normality, not calibrated defect probabilities. The system does not act as an autonomous quality-control authority.

⚖️ Multi-Detector 3-Level Precedence Hierarchy

InspectAI classifies all visual observations using strict precedence rules so ambiguous or borderline results are never mislabeled as confirmed automated failures:

🔵 LEVEL 1: UNCERTAIN REVIEW
Evaluated first. Triggered when |margin| < 3.0 or detectors disagree (e.g. specialized detector triggers while base PaDiM is nominal). Action: Secondary human visual inspection required.
🔴 LEVEL 2: CANDIDATE DEFECTS
Evaluated second. Triggered on strong evidence: high score margin (≥ 5.0) or multi-detector consensus (≥ 3.0). Action: Quarantine component and route to visual QA triage.
🟡 LEVEL 3: POTENTIAL ANOMALIES
Evaluated third. Captures moderate single-detector activations (margin 3.0 to 5.0). Action: Operational monitoring recommended.
